The weekly sections below reproduce the preliminary official course programme. The examiner notes that the plan may be updated.

Exercise numbers refer to Anders Holst and Victor Ufnarovski, Matrix Theory. References T.1-T.8 refer to problems in the [December 2024 home examination](exams/oldexamdec2024.pdf). The listed exercises are deliberately extensive; students should select an appropriate number based on their experience and needs.
